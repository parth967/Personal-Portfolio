# Getting More Visitors to Your Portfolio

Your site is built to **handle high traffic** (caching, fast load) and to **rank well in search**. Real traffic comes from search engines + sharing. Use these steps to get immediate and growing attention.

---

## 1. Submit to search engines (do this first)

- **Google**  
  - Go to [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console).  
  - Add property: your live site URL (e.g. `https://yourportfolio.com`).  
  - Verify (HTML tag or DNS).  
  - Submit sitemap: `https://yourportfolio.com/sitemap.xml`.  
  - Request indexing for the homepage.  
  Result: Google can crawl and show your site for queries like “data engineer Toronto”, “Azure Databricks consultant”, etc.

- **Bing**  
  - [Bing Webmaster Tools](https://www.bing.com/webmasters).  
  - Add site, verify, submit the same sitemap.  
  Result: More search traffic from Bing and sites that use Bing.

---

## 2. Share where your audience is

- **LinkedIn**  
  - Add your portfolio URL to your profile (Website / Contact info).  
  - Post occasionally: “I’ve updated my portfolio with [X]. Link in profile.”  
  - When you apply or message recruiters, include the link.  
  Result: Recruiters and hiring managers click through; shares can bring spikes of visitors.

- **Resume and email signature**  
  - Put the portfolio URL on your resume and in your email signature.  
  Result: Every application and email becomes a chance for a visit.

- **GitHub / Twitter / Slack**  
  - Add the link in your GitHub profile and (if you use them) Twitter bio and relevant Slack profiles.  
  Result: More entry points when people look you up.

---

## 3. What “high traffic” means in practice

- **Billions of users per day** is not realistic for a single personal portfolio; even huge sites don’t see that.  
- **Realistic and valuable:** hundreds to a few thousand visitors per month from:
  - Search (people searching for “data engineer Toronto”, “hire data engineer”, etc.)
  - LinkedIn and resume link clicks
  - Recruiters and hiring managers you’ve shared the link with

Your site is set up so that when traffic grows (from search and sharing), it can **handle it** without slowing down (caching, CDN-friendly headers). Focus on the steps above to get that traffic.

---

## 4. Optional: measure and improve

- You already have **Microsoft Clarity** for heatmaps and sessions.  
- Add **Google Search Console** (above) to see which queries bring clicks and impressions.  
- Optionally add **Google Analytics 4** for visitor counts and demographics.  
- Use the data to refine your copy and keywords (e.g. in title, meta description, headings) to match what people search for.
